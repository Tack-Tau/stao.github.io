TRG is based at the [College of Physics](https://physics.jlu.edu.cn/), [Jilin University](https://www.jlu.edu.cn/), affiliated with the Key Laboratory of Material Simulation Methods & Software of Ministry of Education. We develop computational and machine-learning methods for materials discovery, with a focus on crystal structure prediction, graph neural networks for material property prediction, and generative models for accelerated materials screening.

---

## Open Positions

**We are actively recruiting PhD students and postdoctoral researchers** to join our group. We have openings in the following areas:

- Machine learning for materials science (graph neural networks, generative models, large language models)
- Crystal structure prediction and high-pressure materials design
- Development of machine-learning interatomic potentials
- Molecular crystal modeling and simulation

### What we look for

- **PhD applicants**: Strong background in physics, materials science, chemistry, or computer science. Programming experience (Python, C/C++) is preferred. Prior research experience in computational materials science or machine learning is a plus but not required.
- **Postdoc applicants**: Ph.D. in computational materials science, condensed matter physics, computational chemistry, or a related field. Experience with DFT calculations, molecular dynamics, or machine learning for materials is expected.

### What we offer

- Competitive salary and benefits according to Jilin University standards
- Access to high-performance computing resources
- A supportive and collaborative research environment
- Opportunities to work on cutting-edge problems at the intersection of physics, materials science, and AI
