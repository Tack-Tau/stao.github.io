I am an On-Tenure-Track Full Professor at the [College of Physics](https://physics.jlu.edu.cn/), [Jilin University](https://www.jlu.edu.cn/), where I lead Tao's Research Group (TRG). My research focuses on computational materials science, combining first-principles calculations, crystal structure prediction, and machine learning to discover and design novel functional materials.

I received my Ph.D. in Applied Physics from Rutgers University--Newark under the supervision of [Dr. Li Zhu](https://sites.google.com/view/zhulab), and completed postdoctoral research at the University of North Carolina at Charlotte with [Dr. Qiang Zhu](https://qzhu2017.github.io/).

## Research Interests

My research lies at the intersection of computational physics, materials science, and machine learning:

- **Crystal Structure Prediction** -- High-pressure materials design, silicon allotropes and silicon-based compounds with enhanced optoelectronic properties
- **Machine Learning for Materials** -- Graph neural networks (EOSnet), structural fingerprints (GOM), and generative models for accelerated materials discovery
- **Symmetry-Biased Optimization** -- Novel PES frameworks (ReformPy) that accelerate structural relaxation by bypassing energy barriers
- **Molecular Crystal Modeling** -- Next-generation generative models, large-scale databases, and machine-learning interatomic potentials for molecular crystals
