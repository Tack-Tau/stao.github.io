Using crystal structure prediction algorithms combined with first-principles methods, we predicted a novel I4₁/amd-YSi₆ phase that exhibits superconductivity with a critical temperature of 8.4 K at ambient pressure. This work demonstrates the power of computational approaches in discovering new superconducting materials within the silicon-based compound space.
