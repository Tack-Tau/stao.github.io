We developed ReformPy, a symmetry-biased potential energy surface framework that mixes the real PES with a fingerprint-based energy term. This approach lowers the high-energy barriers that conventional relaxation methods struggle to overcome, enabling efficient escape from local minima. The method demonstrated significant improvements in both efficiency and accuracy when applied to Lennard-Jones clusters, silicon carbide, and other complex systems.
