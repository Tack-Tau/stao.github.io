We applied our EOSnet graph neural network to accelerate the search for direct-gap Si--Ge alloys by performing binary atomic substitution in known silicon allotropes. The machine-learning-guided screening identified promising candidates in hexagonal and orthorhombic structures, demonstrating an efficient workflow for discovering materials with tailored optoelectronic properties.
