We present an accelerated materials discovery framework that combines diffusion-based crystal structure generation with hierarchical screening to identify new rare-earth-transition-metal magnets simultaneously achieving high magnetization and thermodynamic stability. Using this workflow, we systematically explored over 3000 binary (R-T) and ternary (R-T-T') compositions, filtering approximately 240,000 generated structures through machine-learning interatomic potential prescreening and spin-polarized DFT validation. We identify 300+ low-energy magnetic candidates, including 5 thermodynamically stable phases, with the highest saturation magnetization reaching ~1.8 T in Fe-rich systems.
