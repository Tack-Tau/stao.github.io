We computationally predicted a novel silicon allotrope, Cmcm-Si<sub>32</sub>, with a direct bandgap of approximately 1.15 eV -- ideal for solar energy applications. This structure was identified through crystal structure prediction methods and first-principles calculations, and represents a significant advance toward overcoming the efficiency limitations of traditional diamond-phase silicon in photovoltaics.
