We developed EOSnet, a graph neural network that incorporates Gaussian Overlap Matrix (GOM) fingerprints as atom features. By encoding the local geometric environment through overlap integrals of Gaussian-type orbitals, EOSnet captures many-body atomic interactions in a physically motivated, rotationally invariant manner. The model achieves state-of-the-art accuracy in predicting bandgaps and classifying band characteristics across diverse crystal structures.
